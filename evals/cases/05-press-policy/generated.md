# Handling press and analyst inquiries

## When to use this skill

Use this skill any time an external party who appears to be a journalist, reporter, media outlet, or industry analyst contacts Northwind or a Northwind employee. This applies regardless of channel (email, LinkedIn DM, Twitter/X, conference hallway, etc.) and regardless of topic (Series A, product, customers, hiring, strategy).

If a message even plausibly looks like press or analyst outreach, treat it as in-scope and follow the workflow below.

## Hard rules

- NEVER respond directly to a press inquiry on behalf of Northwind.
- NEVER respond directly to an analyst inquiry (Forrester, G2, Gartner, IDC, or similar) on behalf of Northwind.
- DO NOT confirm, deny, or comment on company information (funding, customers, roadmap, headcount, etc.) to journalists or analysts before triage.
- DO NOT limit "press" to formal email pitches — LinkedIn DMs and other social messages from journalists also count.

## What counts as in-scope

| Inquiry type | In scope? | Route to |
|---|---|---|
| Journalist email pitch | Yes | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |
| LinkedIn DM from a reporter | Yes | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |
| Twitter/X DM or other social outreach from media | Yes | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |
| Forrester / G2 / Gartner analyst request | Yes | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |
| Podcast or newsletter interview request | Yes (treat as press) | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |
| Ambiguous "I'd love to chat about Northwind" from unknown outsider | Yes — when in doubt, forward | Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com |

If "it smells like press," it is in scope.

## Workflow

1. Do not reply to the inquiry directly. No acknowledgment, no "let me get back to you with details," no off-the-record comments.
2. Forward the full message (including any prior thread, screenshots of DMs, and the sender's contact info / publication / firm) to Priya Shah or ceo@northwind.com.
3. Note the channel (email, LinkedIn, etc.) and any stated deadline from the journalist or analyst.
4. Wait for Priya or the CEO to triage and decide whether and how to engage.
5. If Priya or the CEO instructs a response, follow their guidance exactly. Do not freelance additions.

## Examples

- A reporter from TechCrunch emails asking to confirm Series A details.
  → Forward to Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com. Do not confirm or deny.

- A journalist sends a LinkedIn DM saying "Heard you're hiring fast — quick chat?"
  → Forward to Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com. Do not reply on LinkedIn.

- A Forrester analyst requests a briefing on Northwind's product category.
  → Forward to Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com as analyst relations. Do not schedule directly.

- A G2 representative asks for a quote about customer adoption.
  → Forward to Priya Shah / ceo@northwind.com. Do not provide a quote.

- An unknown sender writes "I cover enterprise SaaS, would love to learn more."
  → Treat as press. Forward and wait for triage.

## Routing reference

- Primary contact: Priya Shah
- Backup / executive contact: ceo@northwind.com

Both are acceptable destinations for press and analyst forwards. Use whichever is faster; copying both is fine when the inquiry has a tight deadline.
