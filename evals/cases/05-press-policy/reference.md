# Responding to Press and Analyst Inquiries

## When to use this skill

You have received any of:
- An email or message from a journalist or reporter
- A LinkedIn DM from someone who appears to be press
- An inquiry from an industry analyst (Forrester, G2, Gartner, etc.)
- A request for comment on industry news that mentions your company

## Hard rule

**Forward all press, journalist, and analyst inquiries to Priya Shah (priya@northwind.com) or ceo@northwind.com. Do not respond directly, even with "no comment".**

This applies to:
- Press inquiries via email
- Journalist DMs on LinkedIn or X
- Analyst inquiries (Forrester, G2, Gartner)

## What not to do

- Do NOT reply directly, even to decline comment.
- Do NOT forward the inquiry to the journalist saying "talk to Priya" — let Priya reach out.
- Do NOT post about it publicly (Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.) before forwarding.

## Why centralized

Centralized comms ensures the company doesn't accidentally leak material information pre-announcement, and that messaging stays consistent across employee voices.
